Middle: extracted myeloid markers  from a small curated corpus; bold genes are shared by at least one cell type. Right: the joint distribution compares reported label similarity with marker-gene set similarity. Parentheses count cell type pairs among the displayed examples, and marker overlap is measured by Jaccard similarity, $J=|G_1\cap G_2|/|G_1\cup G_2|$.}

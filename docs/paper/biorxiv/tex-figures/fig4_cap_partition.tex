Whiskers are 95\% intervals from 50 cell-type-stratified subsampling replicates and can exclude the observed value. \textbf{C}. Mean recovery for the myeloid analysis and for the all-cell analysis with target groups defined by the study's labels or by the exact myeloid barcodes. Bars are means across the ten cell types and points are cell types. Per-cell-type values shift between the two all-cell definitions, but the means agree.}

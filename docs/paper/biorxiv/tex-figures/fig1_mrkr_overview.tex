\caption{\textbf{\texttt{mrkr} converts reported markers into machine-readable \texttt{.onto} records.} The LLM extracts the supporting sentence and the reported cell type and gene labels, then proposes normalized labels. Exact-string validation accepts \texttt{cDC1} and \texttt{DNGR-1} and rejects \texttt{cDC-1} and \texttt{DNGR1}, which do not occur in the paper. Deterministic lookup maps normalized labels to Ensembl and Cell Ontology identifiers; the LLM does not assign identifiers. The \texttt{.onto} record retains the supporting sentence, reported and normalized labels, and identifiers. Blue denotes the cell type, orange the marker gene, and teal the comparison group of cells.}
